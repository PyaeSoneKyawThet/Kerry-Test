Business Workflow Summary (Project Handover – MPP, 26-06-2026)
    Technical Name: custom_sql_reports

1. Overview
    ========================================
    | Summary Sales Team Performance by Revenue report for Odoo Sales.
    | Provides a summarized view of sales revenue generated by each salesperson.
    | Revenue is grouped by month, Business Unit (BU), Sub Business Unit (SubBU), and currency.
    | Designed for management to monitor overall sales team performance.

2. Key Features
    ========================================
    | Wizard Filters
    |   - Start Date
    |   - End Date
    |   - Multiple Salesperson selection
    |   - Multiple Currency selection
    |   - Multiple Business Unit (BU) selection

    | Revenue Analysis
    |   - Revenue by Salesperson
    |   - Monthly revenue summary
    |   - BU and SubBU analysis
    |   - Multi-currency support

    | Job Order Based Reporting
    |   - Includes Job Orders only (`is_job_order = True`)
    |   - Includes Confirmed and Done orders
    |   - Filters by Job Date

    | Excel Export
    |   - Generates Microsoft Excel (.xls) report
    |   - Includes company information
    |   - Includes report duration
    |   - Includes printed date and currency summary

3. Report Layout
    ========================================
    | Company Name
    | Report Title:
    |   "Summary Sales Team Performance by Revenue"

    | Report Header
    |   - Selected date range
    |   - Currency summary
    |   - Printed date and time

    | Report Columns
    |   - No
    |   - Sale Person
    |   - Job Month
    |   - BU
    |   - SubBU
    |   - Total Amount
    |   - Currency Symbol

4. Column Description
    ========================================

    | No
    |   - Sequential row number.

    | Sale Person
    |   - Salesperson assigned to the customer.

    | Job Month
    |   - Job Order month (`Mon YYYY`).

    | BU
    |   - Product Business Unit.

    | SubBU
    |   - Sub Business Unit from Analytic Account.

    | Total Amount
    |   - Total revenue (`SUM(price_total)`).

    | Currency Symbol
    |   - Currency used for the transaction.

5. Business Benefits
    ========================================
    | Provides a summarized view of sales performance.
    | Helps compare monthly revenue by salesperson.
    | Supports BU/SubBU performance analysis.
    | Supports multi-currency reporting.
    | Useful for management and business performance review.

6. Technical Details
    ========================================
    | Data Source:
    | - Sale Orders (`sale_order`)
    | - Sale Order Lines (`sale_order_line`)
    | - Products & Categories
    | - Analytic Accounts
    | - Currency
    | - Users & Partners

    | Export Format:
    | - Microsoft Excel (.xls)

    | Filtering Behavior:
    | - If filters are empty, all Salespersons, Currencies, and BUs are included automatically.

    | Timezone Support:
    | - Printed date follows the user's timezone setting.
