Business Workflow Summary (Project Handover – PSM, 29-04-2026)
    Technical Name: crm_extends

1. Opportunity Confirmation (CRM Stage)
=========================================
    | Lead is marked as qualified/won.
    | Final requirements (commodity, volume, payment terms) are confirmed.
    | Internal review and approvals completed.

2. Sales Order Preparation
=========================================
    | Quotation is created from the CRM opportunity.
    | Pricing, terms, and customer details are finalized.
    | Sales order is confirmed and locked.

3. Project Kickoff
=======================================
    | Handover from Sales team to Project/Operations team.
    | Kickoff meeting conducted with stakeholders.
    | Project scope, timeline, and deliverables are shared.

4. Documentation Handover
=======================================
    | All supporting documents (PO, contracts, specs) are transferred.
    | Internal notes and CRM history are shared with execution team.

5. Execution Phase
=======================================
    | Operations team starts implementation/delivery.
    | Progress is tracked against agreed milestones.
    | Issues or changes are escalated if needed.

6. Follow-up & Monitoring
=======================================
    | Regular follow-ups are scheduled.
    | Activities are assigned to responsible users/managers.
    | Status updated in system (ongoing/completed).

7. Completion & Closure
=======================================
    | Delivery is completed and confirmed by customer.
    | Final review and sign-off are done.
    | CRM and project records are closed or marked as successful.
